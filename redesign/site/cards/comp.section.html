물리 진술

cavity reservoir에서 무작위 site 하나의 계층 cavity field 법칙을 시행 order parameter로 부호화하고용어, site·bond 삽입의 이웃 spin을 그 독립 사본으로 바꾼다용어. 바꾼 삽입이 정확히 시행 functional이고, 바꾸는 비용은 모든 multioverlap의 집중이 없앤다. 낮은 증분의 선택된 수열과 합치면 pressure의 하한이 시행값의 inf가 된다.

차이

(1) 극한 순서: 고정 $\varepsilon$, 고정 $L$ 의 선택된 수열 위 $\limsup_N$, 그다음 $L\to\infty$, 마지막 $\varepsilon\downarrow0$. shape error의 $\eta$(안쪽 명제 안에서 $L\to\infty$ 뒤에 $\eta\downarrow0$), 개수 절단 $M$, 전개 차수 $q$ 는 안쪽 명제에서 이미 소진되어 조립에는 $\eta$, $M$, $q$ 없는 진술로 들어온다. (2) 이웃 cavity field의 독립은 가정이 아니라 균일 site 표본과 tree 평균 집중에서 증명된다. 국소 tree 구조나 거리 감쇠는 쓰지 않는다. (3) "계층"은 섭동 mark로 만든 보조 계층이고 Parisi parameter는 균등 격자로 고정된다. 보조 계층을 물리적 pure state의 계층으로, $\zeta_{L,N}$ 을 계의 물리적 order parameter로 읽는 것은 증명되지 않는다. (4) 유계·순열 불변 상호작용에 한한다. (5) 하한만 준다.

수학 진술

고정한 각 $N$, $L\ge2$, 선택된 매개변수 벡터에 대해, reservoir root disorder를 평균하면 결정적 시행 법칙 $\zeta_{L,N}\in\mathcal M_L$ 이 있어, 그 label 하나의 spin 과정이 모든 prescribed tree 위에서 reservoir의 균일하게 고른 site와 같은 기대 spin 곱을 갖는다. 서로 다른 trial label은 독립으로 고를 수 있다.

물리 진술

고정한 $N$, $L\ge2$, 선택된 섭동 매개변수마다, reservoir에서 균일하게 고른 site $i$ 의 cavity field $x=\operatorname{arctanh}\langle\sigma_i\rangle$ (깊이 $r=L-1$ 까지의 상태가 주어졌을 때의 자화)의 조건부 법칙을 깊이 방향으로 거꾸로 쌓으면 $(L-1)$단 시행 order parameter $\zeta_{L,N}$용어이 되고, 그 label 하나는 모든 prescribed tree 위에서 reservoir와 같은 spin 곱 기대값 $\mathbb ER_S$ 를 갖는다($\zeta_{L,N}$ 은 reservoir의 root disorder를 평균한 결정적 법칙이다). 서로 다른 label은 독립으로 고를 수 있다.

차이

(1) 부호화 자체는 정확하다. 다만 "상태"와 깊이는 섭동 mark로 만든 보조 계층의 것이고, $\zeta_{L,N}$ 을 계의 물리적 pure state 계층에 따른 order parameter로 읽는 것은 증명되지 않는다(g:encoded-law의 물리 대응 칸, intuition). (2) $\zeta_{L,N}$ 은 $N$, $L$, 선택된 $\mathbf u$ 에 의존하고 극한 order parameter가 아니다. Parisi parameter는 최적화하지 않는다(비교에서 $m_d=d/L$). (3) 보존되는 것은 label 하나의 tree별 기대 spin 곱, 곧 root disorder와 균일 site를 평균한 한 site의 결합 법칙이다. 서로 다른 label은 reservoir 전체의 독립 사본에서 오므로, reservoir의 여러 site의 결합 법칙은 보존되지 않는다. 그 차이는 comp:patterns가 다룬다.

차이

물리 진술

log 전개의 tree 법칙 $Q_k$ 로 뽑은 $k$ 개 replica의 tree 위에서, 모든 multioverlap의 평균 절대 요동을 tree 평균하면 $L\to\infty$ 에서 사라진다. 고정 깊이마다가 아니라 깊이 평균으로만 아는 multioverlap 집중이 log 전개에는 충분하다.

차이

(1) 극한 순서: 각 고정 $L$ 에서 선택된 수열을 따라 $\limsup_N$, 그다음 $L\to\infty$. 증명 안에서는 regularity 매개변수 $\eta$ 를 고정한 채 이 두 극한을 취한 뒤 $\eta\downarrow0$ 한다. $L$ 에 균일한 $N$ 수렴 속도는 없다. (2) 고정 tree마다의 진술이 아니라 $Q_k$ 평균이다: 예외적 깊이의 tree(경계 근처, 서로 가까운 갈라짐)는 확률 $C_k(\eta+L^{-1})$ 로만 처리된다. (3) 기대값은 선택된 매개변수를 고정한 root 와 기대값 표기 ($\mathbb E$, $E_{\mathcal T}$, $\langle\cdot\rangle$, $\mathbb E_{\mathbf u}$)·tree 기대값이다. (4) 극한값이나 overlap이 깊이만의 함수로 수렴한다는 것을 말하지 않는다.

19\begin{lemma}[Recursive law encoding]\label[lemma]{comp:encoding}
20For each fixed $N$, $L\ge2$, and selected parameter vector, with the reservoir
21root disorder averaged, there is a deterministic trial law
22$\zeta_{L,N}\in\mathcal M_L$ whose single-label spin process has the same
23expected spin products on every prescribed tree as a uniformly chosen site
24of the reservoir. Different trial labels can be chosen independently.
25\end{lemma}
26
27\begin{proof}
28Include an independent uniform site $i\in[N]$ in the root variable, along
29with the reservoir root disorder. Sample the tilted reservoir auxiliary
30kernels along each path, and let $\mathscr G_d$ be the sigma-field generated
31by this enlarged root and the full auxiliary prefix through depth $d$.
32At depth $r=L-1$ put
33\[
34 x=\arctanh\angles{\sigma_i},
35\]
36where the brackets denote the leaf Gibbs expectation. This is finite:
37at finite $N$ all configuration weights are strictly positive, so
38$-1<\angles{\sigma_i}<1$.
39Thus $x$ is determined at depth $r$, before the terminal spin transition
40at depth $L$. At each terminal replica leaf, draw a spin with law $q_x$. Given the
41auxiliary prefix, these spins are independent and have exactly the
42one-site law of the independent terminal Gibbs replicas.
43
44Define the random measures backward by
45\[
46 \eta_{r-1}=\operatorname{Law}(x\mid\mathscr G_{r-1}),\qquad
47 \eta_d=\operatorname{Law}(\eta_{d+1}\mid\mathscr G_d)
48 \quad(0\le d<r-1).
49\]
50Then $\eta_d\in\mathcal M_{r-d}$, so
51$\zeta_{L,N}=\operatorname{Law}(\eta_0)\in\mathcal M_L$.
52All state spaces are standard
53Borel, so these conditional probability kernels have measurable versions.
54The auxiliary priors used here may be taken to be finite products of finite
55mark spaces at each realized root; hence the same assertion also follows
56directly by finite sums conditional on the root counts.
57
58Here is an explicit verification for every branching pattern. Fix a
59descendant tree $T$ and prescribe a spin $e_a$ at each terminal leaf.
60At depth $r$, its conditional probability is
61$G_T(x)=\prod_a q_x(e_a)$. At depth $d<r$, let $T_1,\ldots,T_j$ be
62the subtrees rooted at its children. Conditional independence of children
63gives the backward recursion
64\[
65 G_T(\eta_d)=\prod_{\ell=1}^j
66       \int G_{T_\ell}(\nu)\,\eta_d(d\nu).
67\]
68By induction, this probability depends on the full prefix only through
69$\eta_d$. Thus the nested measures preserve the joint output law of every
70descendant tree, including unary stretches and arbitrary numbers of
71children. This recursion is precisely the branching version of
72\eqref{model:chain}. Taking $\zeta_{L,N}=\operatorname{Law}(\eta_0)$
73preserves the root mixture as well. In particular, for every subset $S$
74of the leaves,
75\begin{equation}\label{comp:single-label-moments}
76 \E\prod_{a\in S}\tau^a
77 =\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N\E\prod_{a\in S}\sigma_i^a
78 =\E R_S.
79\end{equation}
80Use independent copies of the entire construction, including the reservoir
81root and uniform site, for different labels. The resulting messages are
82independent of the new physical disorder in the cavity insertions.
83\end{proof}

87\begin{lemma}[Empirical-pattern comparison]\label[lemma]{comp:patterns}
88Fix a prescribed tree with $k$ leaves. Suppose an observable of absolute
89value at most one uses the replica-spin patterns at $a$ independent uniform
90site labels. Replacing these patterns by $a$ independent trial-label patterns
91from \cref{comp:encoding} changes its expected value by at most
92\begin{equation}\label{comp:pattern-error}
93 a\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[k]}\E|R_S-\E R_S|.
94\end{equation}
95The observable may also depend on new insertion disorder, independent of
96the reservoir.
97\end{lemma}
98
99\begin{proof}
100Conditional on the sampled reservoir configurations, the pattern
101$\boldsymbol\sigma_i=(\sigma_i^1,\ldots,\sigma_i^k)$ at a uniform site
102has empirical distribution
103\[
104 \pi(\boldsymbol e)=\frac1N\sum_i
105 \prod_{b=1}^k\frac{1+e_b\sigma_i^b}{2}
106 =2^{-k}\sum_{S\subseteq[k]}R_S\prod_{b\in S}e_b,
107 \qquad \boldsymbol e\in\{-1,1\}^k.
108\]
109The trial-label distribution is $\bar\pi=\E\pi$ by
110\eqref{comp:single-label-moments}. Independent uniform sampling with
111replacement gives $\pi^{\otimes a}$ conditionally in the reservoir; the
112trial distribution is $\bar\pi^{\otimes a}$. The elementary telescoping
113bound for product measures gives
114\[
115 \norm{\pi^{\otimes a}-\bar\pi^{\otimes a}}_{\rm TV}
116 \le a\norm{\pi-\bar\pi}_{\rm TV}
117 \le\frac a2\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[k]}|R_S-\E R_S|.
118\]
119Integrating a function bounded by one costs at most twice total variation.
120Conditioning on the independent insertion disorder proves the last assertion.
121\end{proof}
122
123Let $Q_k$ be the tree law in \cref{tree:log-expansion}. We need the following
124consequence of the decorrelation theorem:
125\begin{equation}\label{comp:shape-error}
126 \lim_{L\to\infty}\limsup_{N\to\infty}
127 \E_{Q_k}\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[k]}
128 \E|R_S-\E R_S|=0\qquad(k\text{ fixed}).
129\end{equation}
130Here and below the inner limit is along the selected fixed-$L$ sequence.
131To see this, fix $0<\eta<1/2$, and let $\mathfrak S_k$ be the finite collection
132of labeled topologies on nonempty subsets of $[k]$. We claim the bound
133\begin{equation}\label{comp:shape-error-bound}
134 \E_{Q_k}\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[k]}
135       \E|R_S-\E R_S|
136 \le C_k\left(\eta+L^{-1}
137       +\sum_{U\in\mathfrak S_k}
138          \bigl(\mathcal A_{L,\eta}\Var(R_U)\bigr)^{1/2}\right).
139\end{equation}
140The average for a singleton has no depth coordinates.
141By \cref{tree:regular-shapes}, nonregular trees
142have probability at most $C_k(\eta+L^{-1})$. On regular trees every induced
143subtree retains the interior and separation conditions on its branching
144depths. For a topology with $a$ branching vertices, each depth assignment
145has probability at most $C_kL^{-a}$. If an induced subtree retains $a'$
146branching vertices, each assignment of its depths has at most $L^{a-a'}$
147extensions to the full tree. Summing out these unused coordinates leaves
148exactly the normalization $L^{-a'}$ in $\mathcal A_{L,\eta}$.
149Cauchy--Schwarz bounds the resulting average of mean absolute deviations
150by $\bigl(\mathcal A_{L,\eta}\Var(R_U)\bigr)^{1/2}$.
151Summing over the finitely many topologies and subsets, and using the bound
152two for each deviation on nonregular trees, proves
153\eqref{comp:shape-error-bound}. Apply \cref{decor:overlap-concentration}
154first at fixed $\eta$, taking the fixed-$L$ limit in $N$ before
155$L\to\infty$, and then let $\eta\downarrow0$ to obtain
156\eqref{comp:shape-error}.

158\subsection{Comparison of the insertion logarithms}
159
160\begin{proposition}\label[proposition]{comp:insertion-comparison}
161For the trial laws of \cref{comp:encoding}, with
162$\mathbf m^{(L)}=(1/L,\ldots,(L-1)/L)$,
163\begin{equation}\label{comp:increment-comparison}
164 \lim_{L\to\infty}\limsup_{N\to\infty}
165 \left|\Delta_N^{L,\mathbf u_{L,N}}
166 -\cB_{L-1}(\zeta_{L,N},\mathbf m^{(L)})\right|=0.
167\end{equation}
168\end{proposition}
169
170\begin{proof}
171For each insertion in \cref{cavity:increment}, replace the reservoir spins
172at its site labels by independent spin processes from \cref{comp:encoding}.
173Denote the resulting trial insertions by $\widetilde A_{\rm s}$ and
174$\widetilde A_{\rm b}$, and their recursions from
175\eqref{tree:Y-recursion} by $\widetilde Y_{0,\rm s}$ and
176$\widetilde Y_{0,\rm b}$. These recursions use the trial kernels.
177
178At the terminal spin transition, averaging $\widetilde A_{\rm s}$ over
179its independent trial spins gives exactly $V_{\rm s}$ in
180\eqref{model:vs}; hence $\widetilde Y_{0,\rm s}=T_0V_{\rm s}$.
181For $\widetilde A_{\rm b}$ the same transition gives a product of
182$K_{\rm b}$ edge factors with disjoint message labels. Given the physical
183disorder and all message prefixes at a depth, these label groups remain
184independent. Their product therefore factors through each conditional
185power mean, successively from the last level to the first. Consequently,
186\[
187 \begin{aligned}
188 \E\log\widetilde Y_{0,\rm b}
189 &=\alpha(p-1)\E\log T_0V_{\rm e},\\
190 \log2+\E\log\widetilde Y_{0,\rm s}
191       -\E\log\widetilde Y_{0,\rm b}
192 &=\cB_{L-1}(\zeta_{L,N},\mathbf m^{(L)}).
193 \end{aligned}
194\]
195By \eqref{cavity:identity}, it remains to compare the reservoir and trial
196insertion logarithms in the stated limits.
197
198Consider either insertion and first restrict its
199Poisson count $K$ to $K\le M$, where $M$ is fixed. Set
200$C=H+BM$, $z=\cosh C$, and $c=\tanh C<1$. On this event both the reservoir
201insertion and its trial version can be written as
202$A=z(1+D)$ with $|D|\le c$, uniformly in all disorder and path variables.
203The deterministic term $\log z$ cancels in the comparison.
204
205At each order $k$ of \cref{tree:log-expansion}, the product of the $k$
206copies of $D$ is a bounded function of replica-spin patterns at at most
207$pM$ labels and of independent insertion disorder. By
208\cref{comp:patterns}, its evaluation error on a fixed tree is bounded by
209$pM\sum_{S\ne\varnothing}\E|R_S-\E R_S|$. Hence
210\eqref{comp:shape-error} makes its tree-averaged error tend to zero in the
211ordered limits. For every fixed $M$, truncating the expansion after $q$
212terms leaves an error at most
213$\sum_{k>q}c^k/k$ on either side, uniformly in $N,L$.
214
215Outside the count cutoff the original insertion logarithms, on both sides,
216are bounded by $H+BK$. Their expected contribution is therefore at most
217$2\E[(H+BK)\mathbf1_{\{K>M\}}]$, which tends to zero as $M\to\infty$.
218This proves convergence of the expected insertion logarithms: explicitly,
219first choose $M$ to control the Poisson tail, then $q$ to control the
220geometric tail, and then take the limits in $N$ and $L$ for the finitely
221many remaining expansion terms. Together with the trial identities above
222and \eqref{cavity:identity}, this proves \eqref{comp:increment-comparison}.
223\end{proof}

226For every bounded permutation-invariant interaction law and bounded field
227law, with the functionals defined in \cref{model:section},

234Write $A=\inf_{r\ge0}\Phi_r$, which is finite by

243The inner limit uses that depth's selected sequence. Letting $L\to\infty$
244in \cref{comp:insertion-comparison} gives
245$A\le\underline F+2\eps$, and then $\eps\downarrow0$ proves the result.
246\end{proof}
