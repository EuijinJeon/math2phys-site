수학 진술

모든 허용 확장 $E$에서 target overlap $R_E$와, 두 target 측 stem 위에서 계산한 $a_{S,e}$ 두 개의 축약 $P_E$의 차이는 $L^2$에서 $2p_N(S^+,e+1)+h_N(S)$ 이하다. 여기서 $h_N(S)\ge0$는 확장 $E$에 무관한 양이고 $L$이 크면 $\mathcal A_{L,\eta}h_N(S)^2\le C_k/L$이다. target I의 사영 벡터는 old I의 $a:=a_{S,e}$와 같다. old II의 $a_{S,e}$를 $b$로 쓴다.

361\paragraph{Projecting the two target sides.}
362In every extension, the marginal target tree consists of two
363ordinary copies of $S^+$, independent below their separation at $d$.
364By \eqref{decor:projection-bound} its multioverlap differs in
365$L^2$ from the contraction of its projected means at $e+1$ by at
366most $2p_N(S^+,e+1)$.  Change the proper child depths back by one,
367then change their evaluation depth from $e+1$ to $e$.  The product
368difference inequality and Lemma~\ref{decor:depth-continuity} show
369that this adds an error at most $h_N(S)$ in $L^2$, with
370\begin{equation}
371 \mathcal A_{L,\eta}h_N(S)^2\le C_k/L.
372 \label{decor:projection-shift-error}
373\end{equation}
374One can take $h_N(S)$ to be the sum of the finitely many
375$L^2(\|\cdot\|_N)$ distances in these comparisons, multiplied by
376a size-dependent constant.  This definition depends only on the
377single-copy marginal shapes, not on the particular extension.
378Thus \eqref{decor:projection-shift-error} holds uniformly for the
379comparisons in the extension sum, even though the union of the
380old and target trees itself need not have regular depths.
381
382Let $a,b$ denote the vectors $a_{S,e}$ on the old stems I and II.
383The target stem in I shares the old prefix through $e$ because it
384contains the anchor, so its final projected vector is $a$.
