수학 진술

모든 고정 labeled topology $S$에 대해 $$\lim_{L\to\infty}\limsup_{N\to\infty}\mathcal A_{L,\eta}\operatorname{Var}(R_S)=0\qquad(\eta>0),$$ 여기서 $\mathcal A_{L,\eta}$는 $S$의 $b(S)$개 branching depth만을 $\eta$-regular 배정에 대해 평균하고, $N$ 극한은 x:decor-limit-order의 수열을 따른다. singleton이면 (평균에 좌표가 없고) 각 고정 $L$에서 $\operatorname{Var}(R_S)\to0$ ($N\to\infty$).

물리 진술

replica들이 계층의 어느 깊이에서 같은 상태를 공유하는지(ultrametric tree의 topology와 갈라짐 깊이)를 정하면, 그 replica들의 모든 multioverlap $R_S$는 요동하지 않는다(self-averaging). 상태 안 clustering(decor:conditional-concentration)과 상태 평균 overlap의 집중(marker anchor 항등식)이 합쳐진 결과다.

차이

(1) 깊이 평균: 고정 깊이 배정마다 $\operatorname{Var}(R_S)\to0$이 아니라 $\eta$-regular 배정에 대한 평균 $\mathcal A_{L,\eta}$가 0으로 간다 (tex 72 "only the $b(S)$ branching depths are averaged", 83 "Averaging over depths is essential"). 원문은 평균이 필수라고 말할 뿐, 점별 진술이 거짓이라고 말하지는 않는다. 다만 증명은 점별 진술을 주지 않는다. (2) 극한 순서: 고정 $L$에서 pert:selection이 고른 수열을 따라 $N\to\infty$, 그다음 $L\to\infty$. $L$-균등한 $N$ 속도는 없다 (decor:order-of-limits). (3) 대상은 perturbed cavity reservoir의 보조 계층(tilted kernel의 prefix)이며 섭동 없는 Gibbs 측도의 pure state 분해가 아니다. (4) pair overlap뿐 아니라 모든 유한 multioverlap에 대한 진술이다(희석 모형에서 필요). 반면 극한값을 식별하거나 overlap이 깊이만의 함수 $q_d$로 수렴한다고 말하지 않는다. 평균 $\mathbb ER_S$ 주위로 집중한다는 것만 말한다. (5) $\operatorname{Var}$는 root(disorder, 선택된 매개변수)와 tree 표본추출 전부에 대한 분산이다.

본문

증명의 뼈대. singleton은 decor:overlap-concentration.s1: 추가 color 없는 spin anchor 항등식이 바로 $\operatorname{Var}(R_S)\to0$을 준다. $|S|=k\ge2$이고 첫 갈라짐이 $e$라 하자. $M=\mathbb E[R_S\mid\mathscr F_{e-1}]$로 분산을 $\operatorname{Var}(R_S)=\mathbb E(R_S-M)^2+\operatorname{Var}(M)$으로 나눈다. decor:overlap-concentration.s2: marker anchor 항등식이 각 고정 $L,e$에서 $\operatorname{Var}(M)\to0$을 준다(상태 사이). decor:overlap-concentration.s3: $G=\mathbf1\cdot\prod_j\mu_{e,T_j}$, $G_-=\mathbf1\cdot\prod_j\mu_{e-1,T_j}$라 하면 $\mathbb E(R_S-G)^2\le p_N(S,e)^2$, $\mathcal A_{L,\eta}\mathbb E(G-G_-)^2\le C_k/L$이고 $G_-$는 $\mathscr F_{e-1}$-가측이다. decor:overlap-concentration.s4: $L^2$ 사영의 최적성으로 $\mathbb E(R_S-M)^2\le2p_N^2+2\mathbb E(G-G_-)^2$이고, fiber bound로 $\limsup_N\mathcal A_{L,\eta}\operatorname{Var}(R_S)\le C_k(L^{-1}+V_{k-1,L}(\eta/2)^{1/2})$. decor:overlap-concentration.s5: decor:conditional-concentration이 $V_{k-1,L}(\eta/2)\to0$을 주므로 끝난다.

72where only the $b(S)$ branching depths are averaged. For a
73singleton, whose average has no coordinates,
74$\operatorname{Var}(R_S)\to0$ as $N\to\infty$ at every fixed $L$.
75\end{theorem}
76
77The proof has two parts. First we control the randomness below a
78sampled prefix through the squared conditional covariance of the
79spin-product vector. This is an induction on the number of leaves;
80delaying the target splits by one level will control its signed
81extension coefficients. We then use a marker anchor to concentrate the
82remaining conditional mean at each fixed depth. Averaging over depths
83is essential when combining the two estimates.

528The covariance estimate controls fluctuations below a sampled prefix.
529We now use a marker anchor to concentrate the prefix projection, and
530combine this with the averaged continuity estimate.

532\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{decor:overlap-concentration}]
533For a singleton, use the spin-anchor identity with no added colors
534and $f=R_S$.  It gives
535$\mathbb ER_S^2=(\mathbb ER_S)^2+o_N(1)$.
536
537Suppose $S$ first splits at $e$ and has proper child shapes
538$T_1,\ldots,T_s$. We will condition one step before $e$. Use $S$ as
539the old tree, designate one of its
540leaves as a marker anchor, and add a full copy of $S$ all of whose
541leaves split from the anchor at $e-1$.  Its first path must be
542fresh at $e-1$, because the old tree has not yet branched there.
543All other target paths lie in the resulting fresh subtree.
544The extension coefficient on both sides of the prescribed-tree
545identity is the same nonzero number
546\begin{equation}
547 C(S,e)=-\delta \kappa(S).
548 \label{decor:marker-coefficient}
549\end{equation}
550The target and old copies are conditionally independent and
551identically distributed below $\mathscr F_{e-1}$. With
552$f=R_S$ and $M=\mathbb E[R_S\mid\mathscr F_{e-1}]$, their product has
553conditional expectation $M^2$. On the anchor-alone side, deleting the
554marker's continuation below $e-1$ integrates a probability kernel to one
555and leaves the ordinary target law $S$, with mean $\mathbb E R_S$.
556The identity therefore gives
557\begin{equation}
558 C(S,e)\bigl(\mathbb EM^2-(\mathbb E R_S)^2\bigr)
559 =C(S,e)\operatorname{Var}(M)=o_N(1),
560 \label{decor:marker-variance-identity}
561\end{equation}
562and hence
563\begin{equation}
564 \qquad \operatorname{Var}(M)\longrightarrow0
565 \quad\text{at each fixed }L,S,e.
566 \label{decor:prefix-projection-concentration}
567\end{equation}
568Division by $C(S,e)$ is made only at fixed $L$; no uniform lower
569bound as $L\to\infty$ is needed.
570
571It remains to control the conditional variance below this prefix.
572The child covariances are evaluated at $e$, which is separated from
573all their branching depths; the move to $e-1$ will use martingale
574continuity instead. Set
575\[
576 G=\mathbf1\cdot\prod_j \mu_{e,T_j},
577 \qquad G_-=\mathbf1\cdot\prod_j \mu_{e-1,T_j}.
578\]
579The latter is $\mathscr F_{e-1}$-measurable.  By
580\eqref{decor:projection-bound},
581$\mathbb E(R_S-G)^2\le p_N(S,e)^2$.
582Keeping all proper child depths fixed, the product difference
583inequality and \eqref{decor:martingale-energy} imply
584\begin{equation}
585 \mathcal A_{L,\eta}\mathbb E(G-G_-)^2\le C_k/L.
586 \label{decor:adjacent-prefix-error}
587\end{equation}
588Here $e$ is itself a ranged coordinate, so the one-step increments
589are summed before division by $L$.  Since conditional expectation
590is the optimal $L^2$ projection,
591\[
592 \mathbb E(R_S-M)^2
593   \le\mathbb E(R_S-G_-)^2
594   \le2p_N(S,e)^2+2\mathbb E(G-G_-)^2.
595\]
596Variance decomposition and
597\eqref{decor:prefix-projection-concentration} now show
598\begin{equation}
599 \limsup_{N\to\infty}
600      \mathcal A_{L,\eta}\operatorname{Var}(R_S)
601 \le C_k\left(L^{-1}+V_{k-1,L}(\eta/2)^{1/2}\right)
602 \quad\text{for all sufficiently large }L.
603 \label{decor:variance-estimate}
604\end{equation}
605In this estimate we used
606$p_N(S,e)^2\le s\sum_jv_N(T_j,e)^{1/2}$ and
607\eqref{decor:fiber-bound} with $\gamma=1/2$: here the full average has
608$D=b(S)$ coordinates because $d$ is absent, while still
609$D_j=b(T_j)+1$. Proposition~\ref{decor:conditional-concentration}
610finishes the proof.
611\end{proof}
