## Lower-bound reproduction attempt

Set $I=\inf_{r\ge0}\Phi_r$ in a bounded permutation-invariant model. For each fixed $L\ge2$ and $\varepsilon>0$, take the **particular** selected sequence in P5, and set $B_{L,N}=\mathcal B_{L-1}(\zeta_{L,N},\mathbf m^{(L)})$. For every point on it,

$$I\le B_{L,N}\le\Delta_N+|B_{L,N}-\Delta_N|\le\underline F+2\varepsilon+|B_{L,N}-\Delta_N|.$$

Take limsup only along that sequence, then $L\to\infty$ while allowing a different sequence at each $L$, invoking P11, then $\varepsilon\downarrow0$. This yields $I\le\underline F$. P3 alone would give only a density comparison and **would not** license replacing $\underline F$ by an arbitrary increment; P5(i) is essential. Nor is an $N$-uniform convergence rate in $L$ used. The fixed-$\eta$ depth average is fed to the $Q_k$ average by P11; the irregular-shape error is removed after the $N,L$ limits by $\eta\downarrow0$. P4's $o(1)$ belongs inside P11's comparison and is uniform in the relevant $L,\mathbf u$ as stated. For the general law, P12 contributes the explicit $2\alpha pD_K+2d_K$ loss before $K\to\infty$.

For the upper direction, P2 gives $F_N\le I$ at each $N$. Thus $\limsup_NF_N\le I\le\liminf_NF_N$ after the reduction. This proves existence of the pressure limit without assuming existence or convergence of $\Delta_N$.

## Definition and presentation open items

1. **The $r=0$ convention should be written as a formula.** L0 says $T_0$ is absent at $r=0$, while the displayed infimum and glossary functional still print $\log T_0V_{\rm s/e}$. A reader can infer $\mathcal B_0=\log2+\mathbb E\log V_{\rm s}-\alpha(p-1)\mathbb E\log V_{\rm e}$, but cannot literally evaluate the displayed expression under the given recursion without that convention. This is a notation gap, not a missing theorem or a break in the lower bound (which uses $L\ge2$).
2. **L1's short notation for insertion factors is usable through P4 and the glossary, but not standalone.** The displayed $Y_{0,\rm s/b}$ are free-entropy insertion ratios, not ordinary partition functions; `g:insertion` defines $Y_L=A$ and its recursion. A brief direct cross-reference near L1 move 2 would prevent the common mistaken reading that $\mathbb E\log Y_0$ is an unweighted Gibbs log ratio at $L\ge2$.
3. **The move-5 conclusion is a theorem invocation.** The charging estimate is named and its relative bound is stated in `g:depth-delay`, but the finite-tree induction and marker variance calculation are not reproduced in L1. That is appropriate for a one-page proof overview because P8 and P9 explicitly state both intermediate and final concentration results. A reader cannot independently audit their internal proof from these four files alone; this is an intended black-box boundary, not a missing external assumption.
4. **The move-6 comparison is likewise a stated bridge, not a consequence of P9 alone.** Its $Q_k$ irregular-shape estimate, analytic truncations, and exact independent-label evaluation are present in P11 and the glossary. Without P11 this would be a real gap; with P11 it is a declared black box whose hypotheses and resulting limit are sufficiently explicit for the lower-bound assembly.
