    §8.1 Encoding a single-site tree¶

    ▸ Lemma 8.1 (Recursive law encoding)¶

    §8.1 ¶1¶

    원문

    s1 For each fixed \(N\), \(L\ge2\), and selected parameter vector, with the reservoir root disorder averaged, there is a deterministic trial law \(\zeta_{L,N}\in\mathcal M_L\) whose single-label spin process has the same expected spin products on every prescribed tree as a uniformly chosen site of the reservoir.

    s2 Different trial labels can be chosen independently.

    ▸ Proof.¶

    §8.1 ¶2¶

    원문

    s1 Include an independent uniform site \(i\in[N]\) in the root variable, along with the reservoir root disorder.

    s2 Sample the tilted reservoir auxiliary kernels along each path, and let \(\mathscr G_d\) be the sigma-field generated by this enlarged root and the full auxiliary prefix through depth \(d\).

    s3 At depth \(r=L-1\) put

    \[ x=\operatorname{arctanh}\langle{\sigma_i}\rangle, \]

    ↳ where the brackets denote the leaf Gibbs expectation.

    s4 This is finite: at finite \(N\) all configuration weights are strictly positive, so \(-1\lt \langle{\sigma_i}\rangle\lt 1\).

    s5 Thus \(x\) is determined at depth \(r\), before the terminal spin transition at depth \(L\).

    s6 At each terminal replica leaf, draw a spin with law \(q_x\).

    s7 Given the auxiliary prefix, these spins are independent and have exactly the one-site law of the independent terminal Gibbs replicas.

    §8.1 ¶3¶

    원문

    s1 Define the random measures backward by

    \[ \eta_{r-1}=\operatorname{Law}(x\mid\mathscr G_{r-1}),\qquad \eta_d=\operatorname{Law}(\eta_{d+1}\mid\mathscr G_d) \quad(0\le d\lt r-1). \]

    s2 Then \(\eta_d\in\mathcal M_{r-d}\), so \(\zeta_{L,N}=\operatorname{Law}(\eta_0)\in\mathcal M_L\).

    s3 All state spaces are standard Borel, so these conditional probability kernels have measurable versions.

    s4 The auxiliary priors used here may be taken to be finite products of finite mark spaces at each realized root; hence the same assertion also follows directly by finite sums conditional on the root counts.

    식 Eq. §8.1 ¶3 #1 항별 표

    §8.1 ¶4¶

    원문

    s1 Here is an explicit verification for every branching pattern.

    s2 Fix a descendant tree \(T\) and prescribe a spin \(e_a\) at each terminal leaf.

    s3 At depth \(r\), its conditional probability is \(G_T(x)=\prod_a q_x(e_a)\).

    s4 At depth \(d\lt r\), let \(T_1,\ldots,T_j\) be the subtrees rooted at its children.

    s5 Conditional independence of children gives the backward recursion

    \[ G_T(\eta_d)=\prod_{\ell=1}^j \int G_{T_\ell}(\nu)\,\eta_d(d\nu). \]

    s6 By induction, this probability depends on the full prefix only through \(\eta_d\).

    s7 Thus the nested measures preserve the joint output law of every descendant tree, including unary stretches and arbitrary numbers of children.

    s8 This recursion is precisely the branching version of (2.7).

    s9 Taking \(\zeta_{L,N}=\operatorname{Law}(\eta_0)\) preserves the root mixture as well.

    s10 In particular, for every subset \(S\) of the leaves,

    \[ \mathbb E\prod_{a\in S}\tau^a =\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N\mathbb E\prod_{a\in S}\sigma_i^a =\mathbb E R_S. \tag{8.2} \]

    s11 Use independent copies of the entire construction, including the reservoir root and uniform site, for different labels.

    s12 The resulting messages are independent of the new physical disorder in the cavity insertions.

    §8.2 From multioverlaps to spin patterns¶

    ▸ Lemma 8.2 (Empirical-pattern comparison)¶

    §8.2 ¶1¶

    원문

    s1 Fix a prescribed tree with \(k\) leaves.

    s2 Suppose an observable of absolute value at most one uses the replica-spin patterns at \(a\) independent uniform site labels.

    s3 Replacing these patterns by \(a\) independent trial-label patterns from Lemma 8.1 changes its expected value by at most

    \[ a\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[k]}\mathbb E|R_S-\mathbb E R_S|. \tag{8.3} \]

    s4 The observable may also depend on new insertion disorder, independent of the reservoir.

    ▸ Proof.¶

    §8.2 ¶2¶

    원문

    s1 Conditional on the sampled reservoir configurations, the pattern \(\boldsymbol\sigma_i=(\sigma_i^1,\ldots,\sigma_i^k)\) at a uniform site has empirical distribution

    \[ \pi(\boldsymbol e)=\frac1N\sum_i \prod_{b=1}^k\frac{1+e_b\sigma_i^b}{2} =2^{-k}\sum_{S\subseteq[k]}R_S\prod_{b\in S}e_b, \qquad \boldsymbol e\in\{-1,1\}^k. \]

    s2 The trial-label distribution is \(\bar\pi=\mathbb E\pi\) by (8.2).

    s3 Independent uniform sampling with replacement gives \(\pi^{\otimes a}\) conditionally in the reservoir; the trial distribution is \(\bar\pi^{\otimes a}\).

    s4 The elementary telescoping bound for product measures gives

    \[ \lVert{\pi^{\otimes a}-\bar\pi^{\otimes a}}\rVert_{\rm TV} \le a\lVert{\pi-\bar\pi}\rVert_{\rm TV} \le\frac a2\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[k]}|R_S-\mathbb E R_S|. \]

    s5 Integrating a function bounded by one costs at most twice total variation.

    s6 Conditioning on the independent insertion disorder proves the last assertion.

    §8.2 ¶3¶

    원문

    s1 Let \(Q_k\) be the tree law in Lemma 3.2.

    s2 We need the following consequence of the decorrelation theorem:

    \[ \lim_{L\to\infty}\limsup_{N\to\infty} \mathbb E_{Q_k}\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[k]} \mathbb E|R_S-\mathbb E R_S|=0\qquad(k\text{ fixed}). \tag{8.4} \]

    s3 Here and below the inner limit is along the selected fixed-\(L\) sequence.

    s4 To see this, fix \(0\lt \eta\lt 1/2\), and let \(\mathfrak S_k\) be the finite collection of labeled topologies on nonempty subsets of \([k]\).

    s5 We claim the bound

    \[ \mathbb E_{Q_k}\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[k]} \mathbb E|R_S-\mathbb E R_S| \le C_k\left(\eta+L^{-1} +\sum_{U\in\mathfrak S_k} \bigl(\mathcal A_{L,\eta}\operatorname{Var}(R_U)\bigr)^{1/2}\right). \tag{8.5} \]

    s6 The average for a singleton has no depth coordinates.

    s7 By Lemma 3.6, nonregular trees have probability at most \(C_k(\eta+L^{-1})\).

    s8 On regular trees every induced subtree retains the interior and separation conditions on its branching depths.

    s9 For a topology with \(a\) branching vertices, each depth assignment has probability at most \(C_kL^{-a}\).

    s10 If an induced subtree retains \(a'\) branching vertices, each assignment of its depths has at most \(L^{a-a'}\) extensions to the full tree.

    s11 Summing out these unused coordinates leaves exactly the normalization \(L^{-a'}\) in \(\mathcal A_{L,\eta}\).

    s12 Cauchy–Schwarz bounds the resulting average of mean absolute deviations by \(\bigl(\mathcal A_{L,\eta}\operatorname{Var}(R_U)\bigr)^{1/2}\).

    s13 Summing over the finitely many topologies and subsets, and using the bound two for each deviation on nonregular trees, proves (8.5).

    s14 Apply Theorem 7.1 first at fixed \(\eta\), taking the fixed-\(L\) limit in \(N\) before \(L\to\infty\), and then let \(\eta\downarrow0\) to obtain (8.4).

    s1 For each insertion in Lemma 5.1, replace the reservoir spins at its site labels by independent spin processes from Lemma 8.1.

    s2 Denote the resulting trial insertions by \(\widetilde A_{\rm s}\) and \(\widetilde A_{\rm b}\), and their recursions from (3.3) by \(\widetilde Y_{0,\rm s}\) and \(\widetilde Y_{0,\rm b}\).

    §8.3 ¶3¶

    원문

    s1 At the terminal spin transition, averaging \(\widetilde A_{\rm s}\) over its independent trial spins gives exactly \(V_{\rm s}\) in (2.8); hence \(\widetilde Y_{0,\rm s}=T_0V_{\rm s}\).

    s2 For \(\widetilde A_{\rm b}\) the same transition gives a product of \(K_{\rm b}\) edge factors with disjoint message labels.

    s3 Given the physical disorder and all message prefixes at a depth, these label groups remain independent.

    s4 Their product therefore factors through each conditional power mean, successively from the last level to the first.

    s5 Consequently,

    \[ \begin{aligned} \mathbb E\log\widetilde Y_{0,\rm b} &=\alpha(p-1)\mathbb E\log T_0V_{\rm e},\\ \log2+\mathbb E\log\widetilde Y_{0,\rm s} -\mathbb E\log\widetilde Y_{0,\rm b} &=\mathcal B_{L-1}(\zeta_{L,N},\mathbf m^{(L)}). \end{aligned} \]

    s6 By (5.5), it remains to compare the reservoir and trial insertion logarithms in the stated limits.

    원문

    §8.3 ¶5¶

    원문

    s1 At each order \(k\) of Lemma 3.2, the product of the \(k\) copies of \(D\) is a bounded function of replica-spin patterns at at most \(pM\) labels and of independent insertion disorder.

    s2 By Lemma 8.2, its evaluation error on a fixed tree is bounded by \(pM\sum_{S\ne\varnothing}\mathbb E|R_S-\mathbb E R_S|\).

    §8.3 ¶6¶

    원문

    s1 Outside the count cutoff the original insertion logarithms, on both sides, are bounded by \(H+BK\).

    s2 Their expected contribution is therefore at most \(2\mathbb E[(H+BK)\mathbf1_{\{K\gt M\}}]\), which tends to zero as \(M\to\infty\).

    ▸ Proof.¶

    §8.3 ¶8¶

    원문

    s1 Write \(A=\inf_{r\ge0}\Phi_r\), which is finite by (2.12).

    s2 Each encoded trial value is at least \(A\), so (8.1) gives, at every fixed \(L\),

    \[ A\le\underline F+2\varepsilon+ \limsup_{N\to\infty} \left|\Delta_N^{L,\mathbf u_{L,N}} -\mathcal B_{L-1}(\zeta_{L,N},\mathbf m^{(L)})\right|. \]

    s3 The inner limit uses that depth's selected sequence.

    s4 Letting \(L\to\infty\) in Proposition 8.3 gives \(A\le\underline F+2\varepsilon\), and then \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) proves the result.
